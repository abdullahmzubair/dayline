Welcome: how this page works

Three small steps, that's all. 1. Brain dump: get everything out of your head. 2. Daily highlight: pick the one thing that would make today feel like a win. 3. Micro-commitment: do one 2-minute action toward a habit.

That's enough for a good day. On a higher-energy day, switch to Full for the timeline, sprint board and projects. Both views show the same data.
